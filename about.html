About Mhlongo Dzonga Bakery

From Our Family Oven in Tzaneen to Your Home

Our Story

Started in 2020 in Tzaneen, Limpopo, we wake up at 4AM every day to bake fresh bread for our community. We use local Tzaneen mangoes, farm butter, and love.

What started as one oven has grown into Tzaneen's favourite bakery!

Our Mission

To bake fresh, affordable, and delicious bread every day using local Limpopo ingredients, while creating jobs for the youth of Tzaneen and serving our community with love and honesty.

Our Vision

To become Limpopo's most loved bakery - known for freshness, quality, and community spirit. We dream of having Mhlongo Dzonga bread in every home in Tzaneen by 2030.
