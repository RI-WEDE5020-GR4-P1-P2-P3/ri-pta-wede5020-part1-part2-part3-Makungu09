About Mhlongo Fresh Bakery

 

Family owned in Tzaneen CBD since 2020. We started with one oven and a dream to bring the taste of Limpopo to every home.

 

We’re proud to use fresh produce from Tzaneen farms - mangoes, macadamias, and more. Every loaf and cake is baked fresh daily with love.

 

Our Mission: To serve the Tzaneen community with affordable, quality baked goods while supporting local farmers.
